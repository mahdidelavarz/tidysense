# Day-0 Onboarding

Status: **Current high-level projection**.

`Login → OTP verification → First Entry / Initial Setup when required → Today`.

First Entry is a one-time setup state, not persistent Quick Create. It may offer manual entry or bounded AI Planning. AI questions only for material ambiguity, produces a structured editable draft and never creates canonical state before review/apply. Failure or unavailable AI preserves manual setup. General provider safeguards, hostile-input isolation and privacy apply; there is no dedicated crisis page/fallback route.

Required states: OTP errors/rate limits, existing/new user, skip/manual path, AI loading/cancellation/invalid output, draft edit, confirmation/conflict and authoritative success. The setup-complete decision remains backend authoritative.
